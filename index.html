BooksLove — Legal

Beautiful, private book tracking for iPhone & iPad.

BooksLove keeps your reading library on your device and in your own private iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. These pages explain exactly how the app treats your data and the rules for using it.

What we promise

  • No account, ever. No sign-up, no password, and no BooksLove server holding your books.
  • Your data, your iCloud. Your library lives on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud. We have no way to read it.
  • No ads. Not now, not later. No banners, no sponsored books, no advertising SDK.
  • Private by default. Your shelves are private from the first launch, and any single book can be hidden from every shareable card.
  • The door is always open. Export your whole library to CSV or JSON at any time, free, and delete everything whenever you want.

The app names every outside service it talks to — including the two third-party SDKs it uses — in Settings → Privacy → Privacy & your data, and section 8 of the Privacy Policy describes each one in full. We do not pretend the app contains zero outside libraries. It contains exactly two, and both are named, with what each one receives.
