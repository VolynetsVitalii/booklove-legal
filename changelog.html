Changelog

What's new

Every released version and what changed in it, newest first. Anything still being built is listed separately at the bottom, without a date - a promised date that slips is worse than no date at all.

Version 1.1.0

Nothing lost

  • Import from Goodreads, The StoryGraph, LibraryThing, Bookmory, Tome, Fable and Hardcover - or from any CSV with a title column
  • A preview of the file before a single book is written, and a plain report of what came across afterwards
  • Fix My Library finds missing dates, page counts and unset shelves, and fixes them in a few taps
  • Import no longer invents a finish date it does not know, so an imported library no longer fills this year’s goal by accident
  • Different editions of the same book merge into one entry instead of piling up as duplicates
  • A reading timer with a focus screen, a daily page target, and an answer to “can I finish this by Friday?”
  • Reading sessions are yours to edit and delete, and the streak is recalculated from them
  • Series tracking with reading order, missing volumes, and a radar for the ones not out yet
  • Saved quotes, typed, pasted, or captured from the page with the camera
  • Spice level and tropes as private tags, your own tabs on a book, and a note of who recommended it
  • Keep scanning to digitize a whole shelf in one batch, or add a book entirely by hand
  • Pick For Me draws your next book from the to-read pile, weighted by mood
  • A card a day worth sharing, and per-book visibility so a single book can stay off every card
  • Covers cached on device, so the library opens instantly and works offline
  • A Privacy screen naming every company your data can reach, an analytics switch, and one screen that erases everything

Version 1.0.1

Released 2026

  • Clearer explanation of why the camera is needed, shown before the system asks for permission
  • Corrected privacy details and licence links in the App Store listing
  • Fixed the support address so it opens a page that actually exists

Version 1.0

The first public release

  • Add books in seconds by scanning a barcode or searching millions of titles
  • Four smart shelves and a cover-first library, in a grid or a list
  • Half-star ratings and private notes on every book
  • A yearly reading goal, a reading streak and Home Screen widgets
  • Statistics, a Yearly Chronicle and shareable Monthly Wrapped cards
  • Goodreads CSV import, and export to CSV or JSON at any time
  • iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad, and full offline use
  • Dark Mode, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver support throughout

Being worked on

Not released yet, and deliberately without a date. This is what the next version is about rather than a complete list.

  • A way to find your next book that leans on your own shelves rather than on a chart of what everyone else read
  • A yearly Wrapped to go with the monthly one, and a friendlier way to look through both
  • Picking the genres you care about while setting the app up, so the first suggestions are not guesses

Found something broken, or want something that isn't here? Support has the address, and every message is read by the person who wrote the app.
