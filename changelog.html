Changelog

What's new

Every released version and what changed in it, newest first. Anything still being built is listed separately at the bottom, without a date — a promised date that slips is worse than no date at all.

Version 1.0.1

Released 2026

  • Clearer explanation of why the camera is needed, shown before the system asks for permission
  • Corrected privacy details and licence links in the App Store listing
  • Fixed the support address so it opens a page that actually exists

Version 1.0

The first public release

  • Add books in seconds by scanning a barcode or searching millions of titles
  • Four smart shelves and a cover-first library, in a grid or a list
  • Half-star ratings and private notes on every book
  • A yearly reading goal, a reading streak and Home Screen widgets
  • Statistics, a Yearly Chronicle and shareable Monthly Wrapped cards
  • Goodreads CSV import, and export to CSV or JSON at any time
  • iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad, and full offline use
  • Dark Mode, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver support throughout

Being worked on

Not released yet, and deliberately without a date. This is what the next version is about rather than a complete list.

  • Importing from six more services beside Goodreads, with a preview before anything is written
  • A reconciliation screen that shows exactly what an import brought across and what still needs attention
  • A reading timer that logs sessions, and a daily page target beside the yearly goal
  • Series tracking with reading order and gaps, and a radar for upcoming volumes
  • Saved quotes, including capturing a page with the camera
  • Per-book visibility, so a single book can be kept off every shareable card

Found something broken, or want something that isn't here? Support has the address, and every message is read by the person who wrote the app.
